Starting the App

💡
First time only Double-click install_and_run.bat — it sets everything up automatically. This takes about 2 minutes on first launch. After that, use the same file every time.
1
Find the app on your tablet

Look for the Cellhub Scanner shortcut on your desktop, or open the Start Menu and search for "Cellhub Scanner". You can also double-click install_and_run.bat inside the app folder.

2
Wait for the app to open

A loading screen appears briefly, then the main window opens maximised — ready to use. You'll see the pink sidebar on the left and the Upload page in the centre.

Processing a Known Bill (everyday use)

Use this workflow for bills from carriers the app already knows: Telkom Mobile, Vodacom, MTN, and Cell C.

1
Tap "Browse PDF"

A file picker window opens. Navigate to where the bill PDF is saved (Downloads, Desktop, OneDrive, etc.) and tap the file once to select it, then tap Open.

2
Check the file name and page count appear

Below the upload box you'll see the file name and how many pages it has (e.g. "48 pages"). If something looks wrong, tap Browse again and choose the correct file.

3
Tap "Process"

The pink progress bar fills as the app reads each page. For an 80-page bill this takes roughly 20–60 seconds depending on the tablet's speed. You'll see a status message like "Extracting page 12 of 80…"

✅

What Happens with an Unrecognised Bill

If you upload a bill from a carrier the app hasn't seen before, it will tell you and open the Gemini Portal automatically.

⚠️
Don't worry — this only happens once per new carrier. After you set it up, the app remembers it forever and processes that carrier's bills automatically from then on.

4

Setting Up New Bill Patterns with Gemini

1
Tap "Open Gemini →"

This opens gemini.google.com in your browser. Sign in with your Google account if prompted.

2
Upload the PDF to Gemini

In the Gemini chat box, tap the + (attachment) button and select the same bill PDF from your files. Wait until Gemini shows a thumbnail of the PDF.

3
Copy the prompt from the app

Back in Cellhub Scanner, tap the Copy Prompt button. This copies a detailed instruction to your clipboard.

4
Paste into Gemini and send

Switch back to the Gemini browser tab. Tap the message box, then press and hold to paste (or use Ctrl+V). Tap the send button. Gemini will think for a few seconds and reply with a block of JSON code.

💡
What does "JSON" mean? It's just a special text format the app understands — it looks like a block of text with lots of curly braces { }. You don't need to understand it, just copy it.
5
Copy Gemini's full reply

Select all of Gemini's response text (tap and hold, then "Select All" or drag the handles to cover everything). Then tap Copy.

6
Paste into the Cellhub Scanner response box

Switch back to the app. Tap inside the large text area in Step 3 (it says "Paste Gemini's JSON response here…") and paste (press and hold → Paste, or Ctrl+V).

7
Tap "Parse Response & Continue →"

The app reads the response and opens the Pattern Review screen showing the column names and patterns Gemini suggested.

8
Review the columns (optional)

You'll see a list of columns like "Date", "Description", "Amount". If a name doesn't look right you can edit it. You can also tap Test next to any pattern to check it finds the right data. If everything looks good, move on.

9
Tap "Save Patterns & Process PDF →"

The app saves the new carrier patterns, then automatically processes your bill. You'll land on the Preview table — same as a known bill from here on.

🎉
Done! This carrier is now saved. Next time you upload a bill from the same carrier, the app will recognise it instantly with no Gemini step needed.
5

Checking Accuracy (Optional)

After processing any bill — even a known one — you can ask Gemini to spot-check a few rows and give you an accuracy score. This is completely optional.

1
On the Preview page, tap "✓ Validate Accuracy"

A small window opens with a 3-step guide, similar to the Gemini Portal.

2
Open Gemini, upload the same PDF, copy and paste the prompt

The prompt is shorter this time — it only asks Gemini to manually copy 3–5 rows from the bill's table. The column names are already filled in for you.

3
Paste Gemini's reply and tap "Run Accuracy Check →"

The app compares what Gemini pulled manually against what the pattern matcher extracted. You get a score and a breakdown by column.

What the score means:

Badge Score What to do
Excellent 90% or above Patterns are working well. Export and upload.
Good 70–89% Most rows are correct. Spot-check a few before exporting.
Fair 50–69% Worth checking the data manually. Contact your IT team to review the pattern.
Poor Below 50% The pattern may not suit this bill format. Contact IT.
4
Tap "Done — View Results"

The dialog closes and the accuracy badge appears on the Preview table header. It will also be recorded on the metadata sheet of your Excel export.

6

Exporting to Excel or CSV

1
Tap "Export →" on the Preview page

Or tap Export in the left sidebar. The Export page opens.

2
Choose your format

Excel (.xlsx) — Best choice. Opens directly in Microsoft Excel, includes a separate metadata sheet with the bill type, date, and accuracy score.
CSV (.csv) — Plain text format that works with any spreadsheet software.

💡
When in doubt, choose Excel. It's easier to share with the team and contains more information.
3
Tap "Browse" to choose where to save the file

A save dialog opens. Navigate to your preferred folder (e.g. Documents > Bills or OneDrive). The file name is pre-filled with the bill type and date — you can change it if needed. Tap Save.

4
Tap the big "Export" button

The file is saved instantly. A blue card appears below the button asking if you want to upload to Google Drive.

Uploading to Google Drive

🔑
First time only: Your IT team needs to set up Google Drive access (takes about 5 minutes). If it hasn't been set up yet, the app will show setup instructions. See your IT administrator.
1
Tap "☁ Upload to Google Drive"

This button appears on both the Export page (after exporting) and the Preview page bottom bar. Either one works.

2
Sign in to Google (first time only)

A browser window opens asking you to sign in to your Google account. Sign in and tap Allow when asked if Cellhub Scanner can access your Drive. The browser will close and you'll return to the app. This only happens once — the app remembers you.

3
Choose the folder in Google Drive

A window shows your Google Drive folders. Tap the arrow next to a folder to expand it and see its sub-folders. Tap the folder where you want to save the file to select it (it highlights).

4
Tap "Upload"

A progress bar shows the upload. When it finishes, a message appears confirming the file is in Google Drive with a link you can tap to open it.

✅
The file is now in Google Drive and accessible to anyone with permission to that folder. You can also tap ☁ Upload to Drive on the Preview page at any time after exporting.

Frequently Asked Questions

The app says "Unknown bill type" — what does that mean?
It means the carrier is new and the app hasn't seen it before. Follow Section 4 to set up the patterns using Gemini. It only needs to be done once. After that, the app will recognise the carrier automatically.
The table doesn't look right — some columns are blank or mixed up.
This can happen if the PDF layout is unusual. Try running the optional accuracy check (Section 5) to get a score. If the accuracy is below 70%, contact your IT team with the PDF so they can improve the pattern for that carrier.
Gemini didn't return JSON — it gave me a message instead.
Sometimes Gemini needs clearer instructions. Try sending it the prompt again and add this line at the end: "Return only valid JSON, starting with { and ending with }. Do not include any other text."
The Google Drive upload button is greyed out.
You need to export the file first. Once you've saved an Excel or CSV file (Section 6), the Upload to Drive button becomes active on both the Export page and the Preview page.
How do I process the next bill after finishing one?
Tap Upload PDF in the left sidebar, then tap Browse and select the new PDF. The app replaces the previous data when you tap Process.
The app is running slowly on a large bill.
Bills over 60 pages can take up to 2 minutes to process. This is normal. Leave the app open and don't tap anything — the progress bar will keep moving. Make sure no other heavy apps are running at the same time.
How do I add a pattern for a carrier I've already set up again?
Tap Pattern Manager in the sidebar. You'll see all saved carriers. You can delete an old pattern there, then re-upload a bill from that carrier to go through the Gemini setup again from scratch.
I accidentally closed the Google sign-in window. What do I do?
Close the Drive upload dialog by tapping Cancel, then reopen it by tapping ☁ Upload to Google Drive again. The sign-in window will open again.
